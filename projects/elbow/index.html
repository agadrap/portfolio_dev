What it is

elbow is a home-cleaning app for iOS. Room-by-room cleaning routines, step-by-step appliance care, stain rescue guides, and household sharing so the people you live with can split the work without arguments about who cleaned the bathroom last.

Private by design

Your cleaning data — the rooms, the routines, the household you share with — sits in your own iCloud. It doesn't touch a server I run. If Apple's iCloud is down, sharing pauses; if I disappear tomorrow, your data doesn't.
