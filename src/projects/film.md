## What it is

A season-based film criticism site built with Eleventy. Each season spotlights one director — handpicked films, full written reviews, and a custom 3-tier rating system:

- **A**nticipation — how much I was looking forward to it
- **E**njoyment — how it landed in the moment
- **R**etrospect — how it sits with me weeks later

## Why

Because I wanted to, not because the numbers work. It's staying exactly that — a hobby, not a business plan. I reckon it's worth keeping at least one thing purely for enjoyment.
