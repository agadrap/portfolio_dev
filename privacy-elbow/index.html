Privacy Policy — elbow

Last updated: July 2026

1. Who we are

elbow ("the App") is a home cleaning management app developed and operated by Aga Drapinska-Tonge. If you have any questions about this policy, you can reach us at hello@byaga.dev.

2. What data the App stores, and where

The App is designed so that we never see your data. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, and we operate no servers of our own.

Data stored on your device and in your personal iCloud (via Apple's CloudKit):

  • Your rooms and how you've named them
  • Your cleaning tasks, schedules and completion history
  • Appliance details you add
  • Vacation mode dates
  • App settings

This data lives in the private database of your own iCloud account. It syncs between your devices through Apple's iCloud service. We cannot access, read or retrieve it — Apple's CloudKit architecture does not give app developers access to users' private databases.

Data stored only on your device: the home-screen widget reads a local summary of your tasks that never leaves your device.

We do not collect:

  • Your name, email address or any personal identifiers
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Usage analytics or crash reports
  • Payment details (processed entirely by Apple — we never see them)

3. Household sharing

The App lets you share your home with one or more household members via Apple's iCloud sharing. If you use this feature:

  • The people you invite can see and edit the shared home's rooms, tasks and history.
  • Participant names shown in the App come from each person's Apple Account and are provided by Apple's sharing system — the App does not ask for or store names itself.
  • You control who is invited, and you or any participant can leave the household at any time from Settings within the App.

Sharing is entirely optional. If you don't invite anyone, your data stays in your private iCloud alone.

4. Third-party services

The App uses no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. Syncing uses Apple's iCloud/CloudKit and purchases are handled by Apple via StoreKit. Please refer to Apple's privacy policy for how Apple processes this data.

5. Data sharing

We do not share, sell or transfer your data to any third party — we have no access to it in the first place. The only sharing that ever happens is the household sharing you initiate yourself (section 3).

6. Data retention and deletion

Your data is stored on your devices and in your iCloud account, under your control:

  • Deleting the App removes the local copy from that device.
  • To delete your synced data from iCloud, go to iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → elbow → Delete Data.
  • To leave a shared household, use Settings within the App; your access to the shared data ends immediately.

We have no copies to delete on our side, because we never had any.

7. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them. It contains no advertising and no public social features.

8. Your rights

Because we do not collect or store any personal data on our servers, there is no personal data for us to provide, correct or delete on request — everything is under your control in your own iCloud. For data held by Apple (iCloud, payment records), please refer to Apple's privacy tools at privacy.apple.com.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version will always be available at byaga.dev/privacy-elbow and within the App under Settings.

10. Contact

For any privacy-related questions, email hello@byaga.dev.
